Our Story

Golfwear for the next generation.

Albatross was started by young golfers with a simple goal: make golf feel more modern, more accessible, and more welcoming.

Why We Started

Built by young golfers.

Albatross was started by myself, aged 18, alongside my brother and a close friend, both aged 16. We created the brand because we believe golf should feel more accessible to everyone, especially younger players.

Golf has tradition, but it should also feel fresh, confident, and easy to be part of. Our aim is to design clothing that looks sharp, feels comfortable, and helps people enjoy the game in their own style.

Whether someone has played for years or is just getting started, Albatross is about making golfwear feel clean, modern, and wearable on and off the course.

Accessible

Modern
